How it works

Simple, safe and discreet

Care without the waiting room. It usually takes just a few minutes to complete your questionnaire.

  1. Choose your treatment

    Find your condition and answer a short, private questionnaire about your health.

  2. A clinician reviews it

    A UK-registered clinician reviews your answers and prescribes only where it is safe and appropriate.

  3. Delivered to your door

    Your treatment is dispensed by a registered pharmacy and sent in plain, unbranded packaging.

Safe care

Designed for safety, not just speed

What you can expect

  • A proper clinical assessment before any prescription
  • Honest advice. If online care is not right for you, we will say so and point you to your GP or another service
  • Medicines supplied through a registered UK pharmacy
  • Your health information is kept secure and private

Not an emergency service

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, such as chest pain, severe bleeding or difficulty breathing, call 999 immediately or go to your nearest A&E.

Tees Online Doctor does not replace your NHS GP. We complement existing care for conditions that are safe to manage online.
